Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription? Open Profile in Mythos and tap Manage subscription, or open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How do I restore a purchase? Open the Mythos Pro screen and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Mythos account and Apple Account.

How do I delete my account and data? Open Profile and choose Delete account. Your data is removed with it. Cancel any subscription first, because deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription.

How do I turn off reminders? In Profile, under Reminders, switch off the daily invitation or the weekly look back. They are off until you turn them on.

My birth chart looks different from another app. Small differences can come from the birth time, the house system and how a source treats an uncertain birth time. Check that your date, time and place of birth are right in Profile.
